## Important year-end note

The dates **26 December 2026** and **2 January 2027** are nominal Saturday positions in the 16-week sequence.

They must be checked against the official university academic calendar before being treated as mandatory synchronous class dates.

If TAU designates a recess or closure, revise the calendar centrally rather than silently compressing two 200-minute topics into one meeting.

## Assessment timeline

### Mid Test

Recommended sequence:

```text
14 Nov — Week 7 closes new pre-UTS material
16–20 Nov — review window
21 Nov — Mid Test
28 Nov — Week 9 resumes new material
```

Keep Week 7 take-home work light enough that it does not compete with Mid Test preparation.

### Final Project

Recommended staged sequence:

```text
19 Dec — Final Project officially announced after Week 12
26 Dec — Week 13 adds exception/contract tools
2 Jan  — Milestone A: OOA + class diagram + responsibility model
9 Jan  — Milestone B: implementation + tests substantially working
16 Jan — Week 16 demo/viva + code freeze for demonstration
23 Jan — final corrected submission / overflow / semester closure
```

If year-end teaching dates change, preserve the **milestone order** even if the dates move.

## Suggested milestone contents

### Milestone A

Students submit or show:

- requirement interpretation;
- candidate classes;
- responsibilities;
- relationships;
- class diagram;
- important invariants/preconditions.

This directly reinforces Week 14.

### Milestone B

Students should have:

- core classes implemented;
- inheritance/polymorphism working;
- exception rules implemented;
- independent + collaboration tests;
- one end-to-end scenario under construction.

Week 15 patterns remain optional and should only be added where they solve a real variation/reuse problem.

### Final

Required:

- final source;
- README/design reasoning;
- diagram;
- executable tests;
- demo/viva;
- ability to explain and modify the solution.

## Contingency use of 23 January

Use the closure slot for one or more of:

- final submission deadline;
- demo overflow;
- students with approved make-up needs;
- technical contingency;
- short final feedback.

Do not introduce a new major OOP topic on the closure date.
